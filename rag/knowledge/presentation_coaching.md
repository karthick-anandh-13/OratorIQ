# Presentation Coaching

## Principles
- Prioritize clear meaning and comfortable communication over artificial perfection.
- Practise in short, repeatable sessions.
- Observe specific behaviours rather than judging the speaker.
- Change one practice target at a time.
- Compare recordings only when the environment and microphone setup are reasonably consistent.

## Practical recommendations
- Organize the message into short sections with one main idea each.
- Mark key words and natural phrase boundaries.
- Record a short passage, review one goal, and repeat it.
- Use comfortable breathing and avoid forcing volume or pitch.
- Ask a trusted listener whether the message was clear and easy to follow.

## Practice Exercise
Record a one-minute explanation of a familiar topic. Review one goal, such as clarity, phrasing, volume, or pace. Repeat the recording and compare the two versions.

## Interpreting automated feedback
Automated labels are estimates. Audio quality, microphone placement, language, accent, and model limitations can affect them. Use feedback as a prompt for review, not a definitive judgment.
