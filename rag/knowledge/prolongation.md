# Possible Prolongation

## What it means
A prolongation is a speech sound held longer than expected in a word. An automated detector may confuse it with emphasis, normal duration, or recording artefacts. A flagged segment is only a possible event.

## Practical recommendations
- Review the audio and its context.
- Keep practice relaxed; avoid forcing a sound to end abruptly.
- Focus on conveying the message comfortably.
- Do not treat an automated label as a diagnosis.

## Practice Exercise
Say a short, comfortable phrase naturally and listen to its overall flow without forcing or suppressing individual sounds.

## When to seek support
For persistent or concerning events, consider consulting a qualified speech-language pathologist.
