# Speech Fluency

## What it means
Fluency concerns aspects of speech flow, including continuity, rhythm, repetitions, prolongations, and blocks. Everyone may experience disfluencies. Audio models can make errors and cannot establish a clinical diagnosis.

## Practical recommendations
- Review marked audio instead of relying only on the predicted label.
- Focus on comfortable communication rather than perfect fluency.
- Practise in a supportive setting and at a comfortable pace.
- Do not blame or interrupt a speaker because of a possible disfluency.

## Practice Exercise
Listen to a short recording and note where speech feels comfortable or difficult to follow. Describe the observation neutrally.

## When to seek support
If concerns cause distress or interfere with communication, a qualified speech-language pathologist can provide individualized assessment and guidance.
