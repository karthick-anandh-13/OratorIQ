# Pitch and Vocal Variation

## What it means
Pitch relates to how high or low a voice sounds. It naturally varies with language, emotion, emphasis, speaker characteristics, and recording conditions. An estimated pitch difference does not by itself mean the voice is incorrect.

## Practical recommendations
- Read a short passage naturally and mark words that deserve emphasis.
- Record the passage and check whether emphasis matches the intended meaning.
- Do not force the voice higher or lower to match a reference.
- Stop and rest if the voice feels strained.

## Practice Exercise
Read three sentences with different meanings. Emphasize one important word in each, record them, and check whether the emphasis sounds natural.
