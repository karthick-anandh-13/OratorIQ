# Possible Sound or Word Repetitions

## What it means
A repetition may involve repeating a sound, part of a word, or a whole word. It may also be ordinary conversational behaviour, emphasis, restarting a sentence, or a recognition error. Review it in context.

## Practical recommendations
- Listen to the whole phrase before interpreting a flagged event.
- Respond patiently and let the speaker finish.
- Focus on the intended message rather than counting every repetition.
- Avoid pressuring someone to speak faster or eliminate every repetition.

## Practice Exercise
Record a short, natural message and listen for overall clarity and comfort. Describe any repetition neutrally rather than treating it as a failure.

## When to seek support
If repetitions are persistent or distressing, a qualified speech-language pathologist can offer assessment and support.
