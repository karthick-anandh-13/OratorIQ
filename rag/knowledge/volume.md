# Vocal Volume and Projection

## What it means
Volume is how loud or soft speech sounds. Appropriate volume depends on the room, microphone, distance, audience, and task. Recorded amplitude is not a direct measure of in-room loudness.

## Practical recommendations
- Check microphone distance and recording level before changing vocal effort.
- Practise speaking so listeners can hear comfortably.
- Avoid shouting or straining the voice.
- Compare recordings at a consistent playback volume.

## Practice Exercise
Read a paragraph at comfortable conversational volume. Record it from a consistent microphone distance and check whether every word is clear without shouting.
