# Pauses and Speech Flow

## What it means
Pauses are normal. Speakers use them to breathe, plan language, separate ideas, and add emphasis. Silence is not automatically a flaw.

## Practical recommendations
- Mark natural phrase boundaries in a short script.
- Pause at punctuation and between major ideas when appropriate.
- Do not remove every pause; allow time to breathe and think.
- Review audio around the timestamp before deciding whether a pause needs attention.

## Practice Exercise
Choose four sentences. Mark a short pause between ideas, read the passage aloud, and listen to whether the pauses help the meaning remain clear.
